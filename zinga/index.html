How it works

1

It floats

A translucent bubble sits on the edge of your screen, over the video. Drag it wherever you like — it snaps to the nearest edge and stays out of the frame.

2

It watches with you

Zinga reads the live caption stream from the player, so it always knows what's on screen right now — and never spoils what's ahead.

3

It answers

Tap to expand. Ask for a recap, "who is that?", or a no-spoiler theory. Pin a note to the exact timestamp and jump back to it anytime.

What's in the bubble

💬

Companion chat

A context-aware assistant that knows the scene you're on. Recaps, character notes, spoiler-safe theories — it works fully offline, or plug in your own LLM backend.

📝

Live captions

Every line streams in, timestamped. Scroll back through dialogue you missed without scrubbing the video.

📌

Timestamped notes

Pin a thought to the exact second. One tap jumps the player right back to that moment.

🔒

Spoiler-safe

Zinga only ever knows up to where you are. It literally can't jump ahead of your playhead.

🧲

Drag & dock

Grab it, fling it, it snaps to an edge and remembers the spot next time — on desktop and touch.

📲

Installable PWA

Add to home screen, works offline, zero build step. The overlay is one self-contained script.

Drop it on any player

Zinga isolates itself in a Shadow DOM, so it won't touch your page's styles. Give it an adapter for your player and you're done.

<!-- 1. include the overlay -->
<script src="zinga.js"></script>

<!-- 2. mount it over your player -->
<script>
  Zinga.mount({
    title: 'S1:E7 "Witness"',
    player: {
      currentTime: () => video.currentTime,
      seek:        (t) => { video.currentTime = t },
      onCaption:   (cb) => track.addEventListener('cuechange', ...),
    },
    // optional: route chat to your own model
    responder: async (msg, ctx) => callYourLLM(msg, ctx)
  })
</script>
